Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 August 2026

Capital Command is software you install and run on a computer you control. It is not a hosted service. This matters for everything below: there is no Capital Command server that your videos, captions, schedule or access tokens are sent to, and no operator of this software can see them.

Who is responsible for your data

Whoever runs an installation of Capital Command controls the data in it. If you installed it, that is you. Nothing in this policy makes anyone else the custodian of what is inside your copy.

What the software stores

  • Access and refresh tokens for the platform accounts you connect, so it can post on your behalf at a scheduled time without asking you to sign in again.
  • Basic profile details of each connected account — display name, username and avatar image — so the interface can show you which account a post is going to.
  • Your media and captions: the long-form video you supply, the clips cut from it, the thumbnails, and the text written for each post.
  • Your schedule: which clip goes to which account, at what time, with which per-post settings.
  • Records of what was posted, including the identifier a platform returns, so the same clip is not published twice.

All of it is written to a folder on the machine running the software, or to storage you have configured with your own credentials. None of it is transmitted anywhere except to the platform APIs you connected, in order to carry out the actions you scheduled.

Data read from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, the software requests three permissions and uses them only as described here.

  • user.info.basic — reads your display name, username and avatar so the app can label the connected account and the posts booked to it. It is stored locally and shown only to you.
  • video.publish — publishes a clip to your profile at the time you scheduled, with the audience, comment, duet, stitch and commercial-content settings you selected for that clip. Immediately before each post, the software re-reads your current posting settings from TikTok so that an option you have since disabled is not used.
  • video.upload — sends a clip to your TikTok inbox as a draft, for you to finish and post yourself.

Information obtained from TikTok is not sold, rented, shared with any third party, used for advertising or profiling, or combined with data from other sources. It is not used to train machine-learning models.

What is never collected

The software has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no usage tracking. It does not collect your contacts, your followers, your direct messages, your location, your payment details or the content of accounts you have not connected.

Third parties

The only services your copy communicates with are the ones you set up: the platform APIs for accounts you connected, and any optional storage or AI provider you configured with your own API key. Each is governed by that provider's own terms, and each receives only what is needed for the action you asked for.

Retention and deletion

Data stays until you remove it. You can delete a stored token from within the software, revoke Capital Command's access in the connected platform's own settings, or delete the data folder entirely — any of these ends the software's access to that account. Deleting local data does not remove posts already published to a platform; those are removed in the platform itself.

Children

Capital Command is not intended for anyone under the age required to hold an account on the platforms it connects to.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version is published on this page with a new date above.
