One long video becomes a week of posts.

CoLateral Marketing is content operations software for creators who publish long-form and want the short-form to follow. You run it on your own machine, connect the accounts you own, and it cuts the clips, writes the captions and books them into a schedule you control.

How it works

Three steps, and the third one is the point: you approve a schedule once and the posts go out without you.

1

Connect your accounts

Sign in to each platform through its own OAuth screen. CoLateral Marketing stores the tokens on the machine you are running it on, and shows you which account each one belongs to.

2

Drop in a long video

A stream, a podcast, a talk. It finds the moments worth cutting, crops them vertical, burns captions, and gives you every clip to keep or throw away.

3

Book the week

Clips go into time slots you set, per platform and per account. From then on the scheduler posts them — you see a calendar, not a to-do list.

Platforms it publishes to

Each one through its official API, with the account holder's own authorisation.

What the TikTok connection does

Connecting TikTok is optional, and it is the account holder who connects it. This is exactly what CoLateral Marketing asks TikTok for and what it does with each permission.

PermissionWhat CoLateral Marketing does with it
user.info.basic Reads the display name, username and avatar of the account you just connected, so the app can show you which TikTok profile a scheduled clip is going to. Nothing else is read from your profile.
video.publish Posts a clip straight to your profile at the time you scheduled it — using the audience, comment, duet, stitch and commercial-disclosure choices you made for that clip before it was booked.
video.upload Sends a clip to your TikTok inbox as a draft instead, if you would rather write the caption and post it from the TikTok app yourself.

You choose the settings per clip

Before anything is booked, the export screen asks who can see the post, whether comments, duet and stitch are allowed, and whether it is commercial content. Nothing is preselected, and the options offered are the ones your account actually has.

Settings you turned off stay off

The choices are read from TikTok immediately before every post. If you have since switched comments off on your account, the clip goes out with comments off, whatever the app was told earlier.

Disconnect and it stops

Revoke CoLateral Marketing in your TikTok settings, or delete the stored token, and it can no longer post. Anything already published stays yours to delete from TikTok.

Running it yourself

CoLateral Marketing is not a hosted service and there is no account to sign up for. It is software you run — on a laptop or a machine you control — so the videos, the schedule and every access token stay on your side.

Your machine, your data

Clips, captions, the queue and the platform tokens live in a folder on the computer running it. There is no CoLateral Marketing server for them to be sent to.

Your own API access

You bring your own credentials for each platform you connect, so the rate limits, the quotas and the review status are yours, not shared with anyone else's.

Nothing is charged

There is no subscription and no payment step. The cost of running it is the machine it runs on and whatever the platforms charge you directly, which today is nothing.
